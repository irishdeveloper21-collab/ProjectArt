About the "Art of Living Together" Drawing Competition

The "Art of Living Together" Drawing Competition is an annual national art initiative organised by Eire Dialogue, created to inspire children across Ireland to explore the values that help build stronger and more inclusive communities.

Each year, the competition focuses on a different theme connected to the principles of living together, encouraging young people to express their thoughts, feelings, and hopes through creativity and imagination.

Through art, children are given a voice to share their understanding of important human values such as kindness, respect, friendship, empathy, equality, and solidarity.

The competition aims not only to celebrate artistic talent, but also to encourage conversations among children, families, schools, and communities about how we can create a society where everyone feels respected, welcomed, and valued.

Together, through art and dialogue, we celebrate the beauty of living together.

Current Theme

This year's theme is "Love"

Love is the invisible thread that connects people beyond all differences. It is not only a feeling; it is an action, a choice to care, to listen, and to understand.

Love

Love turns coexistence into harmony and transforms ordinary moments into meaningful connections.

Why It Matters

Love helps us respect differences, forgive mistakes, build trust, and create safe spaces where everyone feels valued.

Related Values

Love grows with empathy, respect, kindness, solidarity, patience, sharing, support, and belief in diversity.

Your Vision

Students are invited to show what love means to them through friendship, family, community, nature, peace, or humanity.

How to Participate

Registration and artwork upload open together

  1. Register individually or as a class from 1 October 2026.
  2. Upload the drawing through the system between 1 October and 16 November 2026.
  3. Create an original drawing on a white A4 sheet, guided by the theme of Love and living together.
  4. Use accepted materials: painting, markers, pencil, coloured pencils, or pastels.
  5. Include the student details, school details, teacher contact email, and a 50-70 word artwork message during submission.

Competition Rules

Artwork requirements

Eligibility

  • Participation is free of charge.
  • Students under 18 must have written parent or guardian consent.
  • Schools submitting entries confirm that consent has been obtained.

Judging Criteria

  • Respect for the theme: Love and living together.
  • Creativity and originality.
  • Artistic quality, effort, composition, and use of materials.

Accepted Artwork

  • Artwork on a white A4 sheet.
  • Paint, markers, pencil, coloured pencils, and pastels.
  • Original artwork created by the student and uploaded during the open submission period.

Not accepted

  • Digital artwork, printed images, collage, or AI-generated content.
  • Writing on the front of the artwork.
  • Glued elements on the front of the artwork.

Submission Details

The registration and artwork upload system opens on 1 October 2026 and closes on 16 November 2026. Submissions cannot be uploaded after the system closes.

Jury

Judging panel

All drawings will be reviewed by the organising team during the preliminary evaluation period from 17 to 29 November 2026.

The strongest works will then be considered by the jury, with 10 finalists selected from each category and announced between 30 November and 4 December 2026.

Artwork will be reviewed for connection to the theme, creativity, originality, effort, composition, and age-appropriate artistic expression.

Privacy and Consent

Data protection and consent

Personal data will be processed in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

Data collected will be used to administer the competition, contact participants or schools, announce results, and communicate about the competition. Children's full names will not be published online without explicit parental consent.

Copyright remains with the student. By entering, participants grant the organiser a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to reproduce, publish, display, and use the artwork for educational, promotional, exhibition, website, social media, printed, and non-commercial purposes.

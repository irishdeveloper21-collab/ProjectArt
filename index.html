About the Competition

A creative invitation to young artists

Art of Living Together - Love is a drawing competition for students in Ireland, created for educational and creative purposes.

The competition invites young people to express how love connects hearts, cultures, and generations. Through art, students can show empathy, kindness, friendship, family, community, nature, peace, and the everyday beauty of living together.

To be completed by organiser: official organisation name and short organiser description.

Current Theme

"Art of Living Together" is "Love"

Love is the invisible thread that connects people beyond all differences. It is not only a feeling; it is an action, a choice to care, to listen, and to understand.

Love

Love turns coexistence into harmony and transforms ordinary moments into meaningful connections.

Why It Matters

Love helps us respect differences, forgive mistakes, build trust, and create safe spaces where everyone feels valued.

Related Values

Love grows with empathy, respect, kindness, solidarity, patience, sharing, support, and belief in diversity.

Your Vision

Students are invited to show what love means to them through friendship, family, community, nature, peace, or humanity.

How to Participate

Simple steps for schools and families

  1. Register individually or as a class by completing the official registration form before the confirmed registration deadline. To be confirmed by organiser: the source document includes both October 1, 2025 and December 1, 2025.
  2. Create an original drawing on a white A4 sheet, guided by the theme of Love and living together.
  3. Use accepted materials: painting, markers, pencil, coloured pencils, or pastels.
  4. Write the student details, school details, teacher contact email, and a 50-70 word artwork message on the back.
  5. Send the drawing by post before November 1, 2025. To be completed by organiser: postal submission name and address.

Competition Rules

Artwork requirements

Eligibility

  • Participation is free of charge.
  • Students under 18 must have written parent or guardian consent.
  • Schools submitting entries confirm that consent has been obtained.

Judging Criteria

  • Respect for the theme: Love and living together.
  • Creativity and originality.
  • Artistic quality, effort, composition, and use of materials.

Accepted Artwork

  • Artwork on a white A4 sheet.
  • Paint, markers, pencil, coloured pencils, and pastels.
  • Original artwork created by the student.

Not accepted

  • Digital artwork, printed images, collage, or AI-generated content.
  • Writing on the front of the artwork.
  • Glued elements on the front of the artwork.

Back of the drawing

Please include the student first and last name, school name, class or year level, teacher contact email, and a short 50-70 word message about the artwork.

Jury

Artwork will be reviewed for connection to the theme, creativity, originality, effort, composition, and age-appropriate artistic expression.

Privacy and Consent

Data protection and consent

Personal data will be processed in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

Data collected will be used to administer the competition, contact participants or schools, announce results, and communicate about the competition. Children's full names will not be published online without explicit parental consent.

Copyright remains with the student. By entering, participants grant the organiser a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to reproduce, publish, display, and use the artwork for educational, promotional, exhibition, website, social media, printed, and non-commercial purposes.
